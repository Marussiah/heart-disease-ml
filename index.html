// eda findings

Key Findings

📈

Chest Pain Type (cp) — r = +0.43

Strongest positive predictor. Asymptomatic patients had the highest disease rate (~70%) despite having no pain — a counterintuitive but clinically significant finding.

💓

Max Heart Rate (thalach) — r = +0.42

Heart disease patients achieved higher max heart rates. This feature showed the clearest class separation in both histograms and the pairplot.

🏃

Exercise Angina (exang) — r = −0.44

Strongest negative predictor. Over 75% of patients with exercise-induced angina had no heart disease, making it a strong protective signal.

🫀

ST Depression (oldpeak) — r = −0.43

Disease patients clustered near 0. Feature showed heavy right skew — a log1p transformation was applied in preprocessing to reduce skewness.

🔬

Vessels Coloured (ca) — r = −0.41

Patients with 0 coloured vessels had the highest disease rate (~75%). Risk decreased consistently as vessel count increased.

⚠️

723 Duplicate Rows Found

Over 70% of the raw dataset was duplicated. Removing them reduced the dataset from 1,025 to 302 rows — a key preprocessing discovery with major implications for model reliability.

// honest reflection

Limitations & Improvements

⚠️ Limitations

Small Dataset 302 rows after deduplication — wide CV standard deviations reflect limited reliability.
No Hyperparameter Tuning Default configurations used. GridSearchCV on 300 rows risks overfitting to the tuning process.
Gender Imbalance 206 male vs 96 female patients — model may underperform on female patients.
Fixed Threshold (0.5) A lower threshold may be clinically preferable to reduce missed disease cases.
No External Validation Model not tested on data from a different hospital or population.

🔮 Future Improvements

Hyperparameter Tuning Apply RandomizedSearchCV on a larger dataset for reliable optimisation.
Model Stacking Train a meta-model on base model predictions for improved performance.
Threshold Optimisation Select threshold based on clinical cost of False Negatives vs False Positives.
SHAP Explainability Apply SHAP values to explain individual patient predictions for clinical use.
External Validation Test on a separate hospital dataset to measure true generalisability.
